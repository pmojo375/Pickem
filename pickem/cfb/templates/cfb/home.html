How Saturday works

Three steps. No spreadsheet. No excuses.

Start a league

Invite your group with a password or a link. Keep it private — this is for your people, not the internet.

Make your picks

Straight up or against the spread — the commissioner sets the rules. Mark key picks when you want extra points.

Watch it hit

Live scores, standings, and bragging rights update as games go final. The chat can wait until after kickoff.

Straight up or ATS

Pick winners, or play against the spread. Commissioners choose the scoring — the site does not force a format.

Key picks

Mark your confidence games for bonus points. One swing pick can flip a week — or a season.

Live game day

Track your card while the games are on. Green is good. Red means you are explaining yourself later.

Season standings

Week-to-week and full-season ranks, so the trophy is not decided by one lucky Thursday night.

Your group, your rules

Set the slate, key picks, tiebreakers, and scoring. Run the league the way your crew already argues about it.

Built for the tailgate

Make picks from the couch, the lot, or the bar. The board is the same on a phone as it is on a laptop.

Ready to talk picks?

Sign in and get in a league before the next slate drops. Saturdays are better with something on the line.
